## ⚠️ Warnings and Legal Risks

Using executors on iOS—just like on any platform—is **strictly against Roblox's Terms of Service**. Doing so can lead to serious consequences:

- 🚫 **Account Ban** (temporary or permanent)
- 📵 **Device Ban** (hardware ID bans)
- 🐛 **Malware Risks** (many executors come from untrusted sources)
- 🔓 **Jailbreaking/Sideloading**: Often required and can compromise device security

> Proceeding with such tools may void your device warranty or expose you to scams.
